## Motion and accessibility notes
- Route changes use 480 ms edge-to-edge horizontal slides. Home and Shop show a distinct vertical scroll; the nav remains fixed while content moves underneath it.
- Headlines enter in short staggered lifts; UI cards use mild bounce/ease-out. Floating stars/paws are low-amplitude and avoid copy.
- Keep copy inside the site's natural safe margins. Do not place essential copy over an animal's eyes; use a lower photo gradient for the Hook.
- The visible cursor is a simple vector cue, not a device pointer screenshot. Avoid flashes, camera shake and excessive click effects.
- End card details stay still, large and centered after their entrance.
